About Me

Hi, I'm Apil Shah, a grade 12 student at Rupsy International School, studying PCM with Computer Science. I chose this stream because I've always wanted to build things — not just solve problems on paper, but actually create something people can use or enjoy. Outside of academics, you'll usually find me on the football field or lost in a good story — I'm a huge fan of anime, manga, manhwa, and manhua. My goal is to eventually help develop a game — ideally something inspired by the anime and manga I love — and I'm using this time to build the skills that will get me there. I also like stepping out of my comfort zone and trying new things, which is part of why I'm excited about where computer science can take me.
